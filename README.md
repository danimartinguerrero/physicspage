## What I'm building

### [PaperTok](https://github.com/mugar123/papertok)

Scientific discovery often starts before you know what to search for. PaperTok is an open-source app built around that idea: a personalized feed of papers that helps you explore research beyond a specific query.

You can follow authors and topics, save papers to shared reading lists, explore citation maps, and read AI-assisted explanations at different levels of background. The feed brings together sources including arXiv, OpenAlex, and PubMed, with clear labels for publication status and access.

The project has given me practical experience with recommendation systems, scientific metadata, streaming model responses, and the reliability and cost of running AI features for real users.

Built with: React, TypeScript, Cloudflare Workers, and Firebase. Available in English and Spanish.

## How I approach AI and software

I use AI coding tools extensively. Part of the work is learning how to describe a problem well; just as much is deciding how to check the result.

A few principles guide me:

- Test on the actual problem. Benchmarks help me choose what to try. My own workload determines what I use.
- Make changes verifiable. Keep work scoped, review diffs, and check that tests catch the failures they are meant to catch.
- Design for failure. Provider outages, latency, and cost are part of building an AI feature.
- Stay close to users. PaperTok's first users were classmates. Being able to ask them what worked—and what didn't—has shaped the project.
